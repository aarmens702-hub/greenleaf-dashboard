GreenLeaf: a simulated tomato season

Two controllers run the same simulated greenhouse side by side, for days of weather. By default the grower's tuned rules are on the left, and on the right the same rules with the AI co-pilot on top, a reinforcement-learning policy that nudges the rules' equipment settings every 15 minutes. Pick a city, a start day and any two controllers, then press play.

Vertical axis

Dot: a controller's IQM over its runs × seasons; whiskers: its 95% stratified-bootstrap CI on both axes (none for one run). Hollow diamond: the tuned rules, dashed lines at their values. Triangle on an edge: off the scale, its values under the chart. Hours: each season's CO₂, temperature and humidity hours outside their bands, added up.
