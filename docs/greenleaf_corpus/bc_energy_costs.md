# BC Electricity Costs and Energy Context for Greenhouses

Energy is one of the largest and fastest-growing cost categories for greenhouse operations. For BC controlled-environment agriculture, electricity (for lighting, heating, ventilation, and climate control) is a major operating expense.

## BC Hydro rate trends
- BC Hydro rates increased 3.75% effective April 1, 2025, and a further rate adjustment took effect April 1, 2026.
- Despite increases, BC Hydro's cumulative rate increases between 2017-18 and 2026-27 are projected to remain about 12.4% below cumulative inflation, meaning electricity in BC has become cheaper in real (inflation-adjusted) terms over the past decade.
- BC Hydro's residential, commercial, and industrial rates are among the third lowest in North America among major utilities surveyed, and roughly half what Alberta pays.

## Why this matters for greenhouses
- At the national level, greenhouse operating expenses rose 4.3% to 4.0 billion dollars in 2024, with higher electricity costs (up 8.3%) being one of the two main drivers.
- Because energy is both large and volatile, small improvements in energy efficiency (for example, smarter climate control that avoids unnecessary heating or lighting) translate directly into meaningful cost savings.
- Precision climate control that responds to real conditions, rather than running fixed schedules, is one of the clearest levers for reducing greenhouse energy waste.

## Rate structure note
- BC Hydro offers tiered and time-of-day pricing options. Operations that can shift energy use away from peak periods can reduce costs.
- Commercial/business rates apply to operations above certain demand thresholds; large greenhouse operations may fall under general service or industrial rate schedules.

## Relevance to GreenLeaf
GreenLeaf's daily input cost data includes a daily_energy_cost field. The energy price used in GreenLeaf's data (roughly 0.18 CAD/kWh) is consistent with BC commercial electricity pricing. Because energy is a top cost driver and BC rates are rising, GreenLeaf's precision actions that reduce energy waste (responsive heating/venting instead of scheduled) are a direct path to protecting margin. This connects the "is precision worth it?" question to a real, rising cost line.
