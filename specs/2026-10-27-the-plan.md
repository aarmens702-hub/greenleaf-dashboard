## Stage 2: experiments (each an `experiment` issue; VALIDATION only; PROTOCOL §9)
| Id | Question | Arms × seeds | Backend |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | Tune the RB | `rb_tuned` (200 TPE trials on TRAIN_EVAL) | local (~30–90 min) |
| E2 | Does the paper's result reproduce? | `ppo_paper` × 3 vs `rb_default` | Modal |
| E3 | How much of RL's edge survives a tuned baseline? | `ppo` × 5 vs `rb_tuned` | Modal |
| E4 | Residual with fixed penalties | `residual` w ∈ {0.5, 1, 4} × 5 | Modal |
| E5 | Constrained residual | `residual_lag` b ∈ {1.0, 0.5, 0.25} × 5 | Modal |
| E6 | Confirm the chosen configuration | chosen arm × 10 fresh seeds (100–109) | Modal |

The Lagrangian budgets come from `rb_tuned` after E1. E1 must be adopted before E4.
